Why do orders run late?

Edikted · research dashboard

I took 97 daily open-order reports, about 2,850 orders over six months, and joined them into one story: every order crossed with its supplier, fabric and item, with every status change and every pushed date tracked over time. The result is an interactive dashboard and an early-warning flag.

Invoice audit, end to end

Edikted · automation

A pipeline for 5 freight carriers that takes an invoice from the inbox to a final decision without anyone retyping a number. It recomputes every charge from our own data instead of trusting the carrier's figure, runs 20+ validation checks, and hands exceptions to finance in a live web app I built with Next.js, Supabase and Vercel.

PWR House: an AI layer for a fitness studio

In build · MVP Jan 2027

A boutique studio on Arbox wanted to give members more than the membership itself, and to stop building reports by hand. I'm building a layer on top of Arbox, with dashboards for members, coaches and management, automations that replace manual reports and a paid add-on, and later a WhatsApp agent. The goal is retention and less manual work.
